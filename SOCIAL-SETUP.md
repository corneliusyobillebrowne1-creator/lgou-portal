# Opening the college social accounts

Only you can create these (they need your phone, ID checks and acceptance of each service's rules). About 30 minutes in total.

## WhatsApp Business (free app)
1. Install **WhatsApp Business** on the phone with 0880 035 290 (use a separate phone if that number already has personal WhatsApp).
2. Business name: Liberia Gateway Online College. Category: Education.
3. Description: "Online college: certificates, diplomas and AA degrees. Study from your phone. Call 0880 035 290 / 0770 370 287."
4. Set a greeting message and quick replies for: how to apply, fees, how to pay, class times.

## Facebook Page
1. facebook.com/pages/create > Name: Liberia Gateway Online College > Category: Education / College.
2. Add the logo as profile picture, the hero photo as cover, both phone numbers, and the website once you have its address.
3. Post first: welcome post, how to apply in 3 steps, your 65-second promo video.

## TikTok
1. Create a **Business** account (tiktok.com/business), username e.g. @liberiagatewaycollege (if free).
2. Bio: "Study online. Certificates, diplomas & AA degrees. Apply today. 0880 035 290".
3. Post short clips: 15-second "how to apply", a teacher tip, a student story (with permission).

When the accounts exist, paste their links into Admin > Automation & social so they show in the website footer.
Do not share your passwords with anyone, including me.
